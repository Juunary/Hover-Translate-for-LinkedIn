Hover Translate for LinkedIn

Read LinkedIn in any language without leaving the page. Hover over any text and the translation appears next to your cursor.

How it works

  • Rest your cursor on text for 0.2 seconds and the translation appears.
  • Translates the paragraph under your cursor; long paragraphs fall back to the sentence you are pointing at.
  • The bubble never blocks clicks, and disappears the moment you scroll, click, or press Esc.
  • If the text is already in your language, nothing pops up.

17 languages, one click

A small tab sits on the left edge of the screen showing your current language. Click it, pick another, and it applies instantly — the interface text switches too, including right-to-left support for Arabic and Hebrew.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The only thing that leaves your browser is the text you hover over, sent to Google's translation service. The extension runs on linkedin.com and nowhere else. Read the full privacy policy.
